# World Series Watch '26 — repo guide

Beginner-friendly MLB "who's going to the World Series" tracker. Vite + React + TS, static,
deployed to GitHub Pages by `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` (push + nightly cron).

## Data files (public/data/) — all script-owned

- `standings.json` — 30 teams keyed by ESPN id, nested `leagues[AL|NL][East|Central|West]`,
  plus a computed `picture` (division leaders, wild cards, in-hunt, seeds 1-6 per league).
  Records, `playoffPct` (ESPN's odds), `playoffSeed`, `gamesBehind`, magic numbers,
  `runDiff`, `streak`. Regenerated by `node scripts/fetch-data.mjs`.
- `games.json` — today's scoreboard (pre/in/post + scores) for the "Today's games" strip.

## Conventions

- Team ids are ESPN MLB team ids. League/division come from the standings tree.
- Playoff field is computed canonically: 3 division leaders (best record per division,
  seeds 1-3) + 3 wild cards (next-best 3 non-leaders, seeds 4-6) per league. A wild card
  can out-record a division leader but is always seeded below — this is a deliberate
  teaching point in the UI (`explain.ts`, the "wild-card twist" card).
- The whole audience is baseball newcomers: every jargon term goes through `<Term>`
  (glossary in `src/lib/explain.ts`); prefer plain-language status ("2 games behind the
  last playoff spot") over raw numbers.
- Picks (AL champ / NL champ / WS winner) live in localStorage + `#p=<base64url>`; no backend.
- No media curation and no Claude routine — the nightly Actions cron runs fetch-data,
  commits, and redeploys on its own.

## Playoff structure (for reference)

Wild Card round (best of 3, seeds 3v6 & 4v5) → Division Series (best of 5, seeds 1 & 2 enter
here off a bye) → League Championship (best of 7) → World Series (best of 7, AL champ vs NL champ).
